Use AI to research a topic, analyse data and draft reports, and learn how to check what it gives you. No coding needed.

Research, analysis and reports take hours. With the right method, AI can cut that dramatically, if you know how to verify what it tells you. Learn the workflow and the checks that keep it trustworthy.
