Learn to turn a business question into a clear answer: understand your data, summarise it honestly and present what it says.

Numbers do not speak for themselves. This course gives you a reliable routine for every analysis: define the question, check the data, summarise it and say what it means. We work in a spreadsheet, so you can use it on Monday.
