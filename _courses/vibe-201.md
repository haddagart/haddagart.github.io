Take an idea all the way to a live web application you can share, using AI for the code and your own judgement for the decisions.

A prototype on your screen is not yet something people can use. Learn how to take an AI-built idea to a live web app: structure, data, accounts and a public address, with clear rules about what to leave to the AI and what to decide yourself.
