Pick a real internal need, such as a tracker, a calculator or a small dashboard, and build and launch it in the workshop, with your team doing the building.

The best way to learn is to build something you will really use. Bring a real need, and your team will plan, build and launch an internal tool during the workshop, with guidance at each step.
