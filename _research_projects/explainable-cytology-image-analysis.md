This project looked at how to analyse cells in breast cytology slides, where low contrast, fuzzy borders and overlapping cells make segmentation hard. It began with a master's thesis combining neuro-fuzzy systems with ant colony optimisation for segmentation and classification, and continued with a controllable semi-automated approach that revisits classic segmentation techniques, as an alternative to end-to-end deep learning that lacks explainability by design.
