Human tracking in video sequences is a building block for many applications, including human action recognition, attack and anomaly detection, and suspect tracking. To understand what people do in a video, you first have to follow them through it. Existing work covers both real-time approaches and approaches that ignore the real-time constraint.

Tracking a person means locating them in space and in time, since video is a spatio-temporal medium. The main challenge in this area is finding a good compromise between response time and tracking accuracy.

The goal of this project was to implement an intelligent model for real-time human tracking in video sequences, using a hybrid of machine learning and interpolation techniques guided by bio-inspired algorithms.
