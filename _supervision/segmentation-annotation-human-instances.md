Detecting people in still or moving scenes is crucial for open problems such as video understanding, anomaly detection, compression and restoration. Segmentation retrieves representative human instances and extracts the regions of interest, so that solutions can focus on how people behave in their environment.

Annotation complements segmentation by matching the regions of interest found in each image. The major challenge is matching instances correctly despite the difficulties that come with collecting an image sequence.

The objective of this project was to implement an intelligent model that segments regions representing people in image sequences and to connect it with a bio-inspired module for region matching.
