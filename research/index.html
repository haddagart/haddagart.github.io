What I work on

I'm a Ph.D. student in Artificial Intelligence at the SIMPA (Signal, Image, Parole) Laboratory, University of Sciences and Technology of Oran - Mohamed Boudiaf (USTOMB). My thesis explores Human Action Recognition in Video Sequences using Bio-inspired Models: how far can brain-inspired computation (spiking neurons, direction-selective motion processing, event-based sensing) go in understanding what happens in a video, at a fraction of the energy of conventional deep learning?
